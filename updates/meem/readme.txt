Hey, Vincy! If you're reading this, you've probably received my birthday gift for you this year. So basically the gist is that I have been feeling guilty as all hell these past few years for basically never getting you anything for your birthdays, so I started work on this some time ago. 

I've lost count of exactly how many lines of dialogue Medic has, but there's at least a couple of them; you can toggle his speech or change how often he'll say something in his menu. He'll develop more of a relationship with you as you have him around on your computer for longer and interact with him positively, though I might add new, less linear interactions with him at some point in the future. He's also able to:
- keep a calendar for you,
- open RSS feeds,
- check your email,
- empty your recycle bin,
- fix your PC's clock, and
- generally hang around whining every once in a while about how much you inconvenience him! Isn't that nifty?

Will not lie, this was more than a bit of a pain in the ass to make, but it's worth it to make up for all the years of nothing I think. His hotkeys are at the bottom of this message. Happy FREAKING birthday, man!

Hotkeys:
T - say random dialogue
R - repeat last dialogue
F1 - open this file again
To set up your email for him to read, you can open SSP's Preferences menu either by right-clicking him and selecting "Options > Preferences", or by asking him to do it. Under the POP menu, click Add... and enter your details. Stuff like POP depends on your provider, so you'll need to Google that on your own I am afraid.
